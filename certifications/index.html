What each document can establish

  • A facility certificate relates to the named facility and its stated scope; it does not automatically certify every product.
  • A product certificate or test report must be matched to the exact formula, product identity and relevant market.
  • An SDS, COA or supplier declaration has a defined purpose and does not by itself establish blanket market authorization.
  • Water permeability, wudu suitability, halal status and destination-market compliance are separate questions requiring product-specific review.
