Creator collaboration FAQ

Can a nail creator start with samples before a private label order?

Yes. Creators can send a product brief and request sample options first. MOQ, price, documents and packaging are confirmed for the selected formula and project.

What should a creator include in a collaboration inquiry?

Include your social profile or brand link, product category, target country, audience or sales channel, approximate quantity, packaging direction and the content or product-launch timeline.

Do you provide free products or paid influencer sponsorships?

This page is for product development and OEM/ODM collaboration inquiries. Sample availability, shipping, commercial terms and any content arrangement must be reviewed case by case in writing.

Which creator projects are a good fit?

Good-fit projects include nail artists developing signature colors, educators building training kits, TikTok or Instagram creators testing a product line, and indie beauty brands preparing a first private label collection.
