How the sample request works

1. Choose 3 categories

Select up to three different categories: Gel Polish, Builder Gel, Acrylic and Nail Art. You can tell us the product name, color direction or application you want to test.

2. Tell us your direction

Social profile, country, product direction and content plan are optional. Sharing them is helpful because it lets us understand your market and recommend a better sample set.

3. Confirm the request

We review sample availability and shipping details for your selected products. If you move forward, product specifications and commercial terms are confirmed in writing, including a PI where applicable.

What to include in your message

  • Your name or brand name and email address;
  • The up to 3 categories or specific products you want to test;
  • Your country and sales or content market, if you would like to share it;
  • Your TikTok, Instagram, YouTube, website or store link, if available;
  • Your product idea, preferred colors and intended content or launch plan, if available.
No forced creator profile: Social links and content plans are welcome but not required. The sample request can start with the product direction alone.

Creator Sample Program FAQ

How many free samples can one customer request?

Each customer can request up to 3 free product samples from 3 different product categories. Sample shipping and any project-specific costs should be confirmed with the product team.

Which categories are available?

Gel Polish, Builder Gel, Acrylic and Nail Art products are available for sample requests, subject to the selected product and project confirmation.

Do I need to provide my social link?

No. Social links, country, product direction and content plans are optional, but sharing them helps us recommend a more relevant sample set.
