2. Formulation Chemistry: Solving the Pastel Streakiness Dilemma

In conventional gel polish formulations, pastel colors are notorious among salon technicians for being difficult to apply. Because pastels require heavy white titanium dioxide ($ ext{TiO}_2$) pigment loadings to achieve opacity, ordinary formulations frequently suffer from:

  • Brush-Stroke Streaking: Visible lines and drag marks when dragging the brush across the nail plate.
  • Pigment Settling & Separation: Heavy pigment particles sink to the bottom of the bottle after 2–3 weeks of shelf storage, requiring vigorous shaking that causes micro-bubbles.
  • Cuticle Flooding & Shrinkage: Low-viscosity formulas run into lateral sidewalls and pull back from free edges during flash curing.

GZ NEWNAIL's Technical Solution — Thixotropic Solid Butter Chemistry:
Our R&D laboratory engineered a proprietary smart rheology network. Under the shear stress of the salon brush, the gel instantly thins to provide butter-like, streak-free single-stroke glide. Once the brush is lifted, the formula instantly restores its structured viscosity, self-leveling into an immaculate glass-smooth dome without running or shrinking.

4. Sunscreen Chemistry: Preventing Spring/Summer UV Yellowing

As UV radiation intensity surges throughout spring and summer, light pastel gels and sheer milky BIAB overlays face a critical vulnerability: photo-oxidative discoloration. Traditional nitrocellulose and low-grade aromatic acrylate top coats degrade under sunlight, turning pastel yellows into dingy brownish tones and milky whites into murky yellow within 7–10 days.

🛡️ GZ NEWNAIL Triple Optical Sunscreen Defense:

1. Hindered Amine Light Stabilizers (HALS): High-efficiency radical scavengers permanently quench photo-degradation reaction chains induced by solar UV exposure.
2. Hydroxyphenyl-Benzotriazole UV Absorbers: Selectively filters 300–385nm solar ultraviolet rays, shielding the underlying pastel pigments.
3. Optical Violet Tone Equalizers: Sub-micron violet optical brighteners actively counteract any yellow reflection, maintaining brilliant ice-crystal clarity over 35+ days of salon wear.
4. 100% 2026 EU TPO-Ban Compliant: Formulated strictly with non-toxic TPO-L and BAPO photoinitiators with 0% banned CMR 1B substances and 100% HEMA-Free resin.
