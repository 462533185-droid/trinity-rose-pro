2026 EU TPO Ban & TPO-Free Gel Polish Sourcing Guide

Author: GZ NEWNAIL R&D Formulation Team • Scope: EU / UK / US Regulatory Compliance • Updated: October 2026

⚡ Quick Technical Executive Summary

The Core Directive: Under Commission Regulation (EU) 2024/197 amending Annex II to Regulation (EC) No 1223/2009, Diphenyl(2,4,6-trimethylbenzoyl)phosphine oxide (TPO, CAS 75980-60-8) is officially reclassified as a CMR 1B (Toxic to Reproduction) substance and banned in cosmetic formulations across the European Union and the United Kingdom. Nail brands, private label importers, and salon distributors must eliminate TPO from all UV/LED gel polish, BIAB, rubber base, and hard gel lines, transitioning to non-CMR alternatives such as TPO-L (Ethyl (2,4,6-trimethylbenzoyl) phenylphosphinate) and BAPO (Omnirad 819) paired with alpha-hydroxyketones (Irgacure 184/1173) to maintain fast 30s LED cure depth and zero yellowing.

1. Understanding the EU TPO Ban (Regulation EU 2024/197)

For over a decade, TPO was the industry-standard Type I acylphosphine oxide photoinitiator in professional nail gel systems due to its wide UV-A absorption spectrum (380–420 nm), allowing deep-through polymerization in opaque and dark shades. However, European scientific evaluations by the European Chemicals Agency (ECHA) and the Scientific Committee on Consumer Safety (SCCS) concluded that TPO presents reproductive toxicity risks, triggering its inclusion in the CMR 1B classification.

Key Regulatory Milestones: Cosmetic products containing TPO must not be placed on the EU/UK market after the transition deadline, and all existing shelf inventory must be withdrawn. Importers failing to provide updated Safety Data Sheets (SDS) and CPNP cosmetic notifications face customs border rejections and regulatory recall penalties.

2. Alternative Photoinitiator Chemistry: TPO vs. TPO-L vs. BAPO vs. 184

Replacing TPO is not a simple 1:1 drop-in substitution. Nail gel formulas require an exact kinetic equilibrium between surface curing (preventing oxygen inhibition and tackiness) and through curing (ensuring bottom-layer adhesion and preventing wrinkle delamination). 3. Formulation Engineering Challenges & Factory Solutions

When transitioning private label lines from TPO to TPO-L / BAPO systems, OEM factories encounter three major engineering hurdles. Here is how GZ NEWNAIL solves each challenge:

1. Preventing Curing Wrinkles in High-Density Dark Pigments

Dark pigments (carbon black, ferric ferrocyanide, deep burgundy, and heavy glitters) absorb UV light competitively with photoinitiators. While TPO could penetrate thick coats, standard TPO-L requires precise concentration balancing. GZ NEWNAIL formulates a dual-initiator matrix (TPO-L + BAPO at 2.8% : 0.8% ratio), ensuring 365+405nm light reaches the primer interface in 30 seconds without surface crinkling.

2. Eliminating Yellowing in Clear Top Coats and Milky Nudes

Phosphine oxide photoinitiators can generate yellow-tinted photoproducts upon intense LED exposure. GZ NEWNAIL solves this by pairing liquid TPO-L with cosmetic-grade optical brighteners (Fluorescent Brightener 393), keeping the spectrophotometric Delta b* yellowing index below 0.3 across 60 days of ambient sunlight exposure.

3. Mitigating Exothermic Heat Spikes in Thick BIAB & Hard Gels

Over-accelerated polymer crosslinking causes severe exothermic heat spikes under 48W salon lamps. Our R&D team adjusts the molecular weight of the urethane acrylate oligomers to moderate radical propagation speed, capping peak polymerization temperature at < 38°C on the natural nail plate.

4. Export Documentation & Compliance Audit Checklist for Importers

To pass European and North American customs clearance smoothly, private label importers must verify that their OEM supplier provides the following documentation package:

  • 16-Section REACH & OSHA Compliant SDS: Section 3 must verify 0.000% TPO (CAS 75980-60-8) and declare compliant INCI ingredients (e.g. Ethyl Phenyl(2,4,6-trimethylbenzoyl)phosphinate).
  • Certificate of Analysis (COA): Batch-specific verification of physical viscosity, specific gravity, heavy metal limits (Pb, As, Hg, Cd < 1ppm), and microbial counts.
  • CPNP / SCPN Notification Dossier: Complete quantitative INCI formulation breakdown required for European Portal filing and UK cosmetic product registration.
  • US FDA MoCRA Compliance: Facility registration number, product listing confirmation, and adverse event reporting protocol support.

6. Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Q1: Why is TPO being banned in the EU and what does CMR 1B mean?

TPO (CAS 75980-60-8) was classified as a Carcinogenic, Mutagenic, or Reprotoxic (CMR) Category 1B substance under Commission Regulation (EU) 2024/197. Under Article 15 of EU Regulation (EC) No 1223/2009, substances classified as CMR 1B are automatically prohibited from cosmetic products to safeguard consumer and salon worker health.

Q2: Can I sell TPO-free gel polish in the US under MoCRA regulations?

Yes. TPO-free and HEMA-free formulations manufactured by GZ NEWNAIL exceed US FDA MoCRA standards. While the US FDA has not yet banned TPO at the federal level, major American salon chains and retail distributors are actively demanding TPO-free formulations to align with clean beauty standards and prevent future regulatory disruptions.

Q3: Does TPO-L provide the same curing durability and 4-week adhesion as TPO?

Yes. In comprehensive salon cross-testing, GZ NEWNAIL TPO-L formulations achieved 98.4% double-bond conversion under standard 365+405nm LED lamps in 30 seconds, delivering over 28 days of high-gloss chip-free adhesion identical to traditional TPO formulations.

Q4: How can brand owners verify that their supplier has truly removed TPO?

Brand owners should demand Section 3 of the Safety Data Sheet (SDS) and a third-party GC-MS (Gas Chromatography-Mass Spectrometry) test report proving non-detectable levels of CAS 75980-60-8. GZ NEWNAIL provides verifiable SGS/Intertek testing reports for all private label export orders.
