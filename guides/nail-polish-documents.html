Water-Based Nail Polish COA — WB817

Manufacturer-issued certificate of analysis supplied for water-based nail polish: model WB817, lot 2026082001, quantity 5 kg, delivery date 21 August 2026, stated shelf life 1 year.

The report records appearance, smell, specific gravity (labelled “proportion”), pH, suspension and color-paste compatibility. It reports a value of 0.99 for specific gravity, pH 7, and an overall PASS conclusion. These are the manufacturer’s reported results for this identified batch, not independently repeated testing.

This COA is not a Halal certificate, a complete ingredient list or evidence for every nail polish or gel polish batch. Match your order to its product code, batch and agreed specification.
