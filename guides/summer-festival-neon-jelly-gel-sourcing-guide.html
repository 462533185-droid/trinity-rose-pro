Infused with Hindered Amine Light Stabilizers (HALS) and dual Benzotriazole UV absorbers. Blocks 99.4% of degrading UV rays, protecting pastel and neon hues from yellowing while resisting beach sand scratching.

Regulatory Standard

100% 2026 EU TPO-Ban Compliant

Completely free of banned photoinitiator TPO (CMR 1B). Formulated with safe TPO-L and BAPO complexes for deep, full-depth curing under 365+405nm LED lamps with zero exothermic heat spikes.

💡 Factory Tech Insight: Why Summer Gels Yellow (And How We Stop It)

Exposure to sunlight triggers photo-oxidation in aromatic urethane oligomers, resulting in quinoid yellow chromophores. GZ NEWNAIL utilizes 100% aliphatic oligomer backbones combined with violet optical brighteners (CI 60725) to neutralize yellow shifts and maintain crystal-clear brilliance under intense tropical sun.

Q1: Why must overseas salon brands finalize Summer 2027 festival and resort gel orders by November/December 2026?

Taking into account 7-14 days for private label custom bottle silkscreening and formulation, 30-45 days for ocean freight, and crucially the 3-4 week Chinese Spring Festival (CNY) factory shutdown in January/February 2027, ordering in November/December guarantees pre-CNY dispatch and avoids massive spring supply shortages.

Q2: How does GZ NEWNAIL solve the common streakiness and poor opacity issues in neon fluorescent gel polishes?

Traditional neon polishes often suffer from chalkiness, uneven pigment sedimentation, and require 3-4 coats for coverage. GZ NEWNAIL uses high-luminescence micro-milled organic fluorescent pigments treated with specialized polymeric wetting dispersants. This creates a thixotropic, self-leveling cream matrix that delivers vibrant, opaque, streak-free color in just one or two thin brush strokes.

Q3: What makes the Poolproof Rubber Base resistant to lifting during beach vacations and chlorinated swimming?

Our Poolproof Rubber Base is engineered with a high-density, cross-linked elastomeric polyurethane acrylate oligomer network. It exhibits superior hydrophobic barrier properties that resist water osmotic swelling and chlorine bond degradation. The flexible matrix expands and contracts naturally with the keratin plate, preventing perimeter edge lifting even after 4+ hours of daily water immersion.

Q4: How does the Diamond Non-Yellowing Top Coat protect summer neon and translucent jelly gels from sun damage?

Summer UV radiation and chlorine commonly cause light neon and jelly gels to turn murky yellowish or dull. Our UV-Defense Tempered Top Coat incorporates dual Hindered Amine Light Stabilizers (HALS) and nano-scale Benzotriazole UV absorbers. Together with a silicone-modified scratch-resistant resin, it filters 99.4% of degrading UV rays and resists beach sand abrasion, preserving mirror gloss for 35+ days.
