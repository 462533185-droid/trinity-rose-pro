Private Label & Bulk Sourcing Questions

How do I request a private label nail product quote?

Send the product type, required quantity, shades, bottle size, logo artwork, packaging requirements and destination country. Confirm the specification and sample before approving production.

How is the minimum order quantity confirmed?

MOQ depends on the product, formula, shades and packaging. Request a written quotation for your exact specification; a minimum shown for one product does not apply to every item.

What should I check before approving a sample?

Check shade, application behavior, viscosity, brush, bottle closure, label artwork and packaging. Record the sample ID and approved revision so production can follow the same specification.

Which documents should I request?

Ask for documents that match your chosen product and formula, such as the ingredient list, SDS and batch COA where available. Review the document scope and dates; the document gallery explains the supplied facility certificate and registration document.

How is bottled gel polish packed?

Our standard bottled gel polish packing is 360 pieces per master carton. Confirm the packing specification for your order; bulk containers, kits and other products can require different packing.

    Nail Prep, Remover & Cleaner

    15ml Magic Remover Gel

    Factory Price RangeUS$0.35 - 0.90 / bottle
    Reference price by bottle, label, formula and order quantity.

    A fast brush-on solution developed to help remove UV gel polish efficiently. After the top coat is lightly filed, the remover helps the cured gel layer lift and peel in approximately 1-3 minutes. It is designed for professional nail salons, nail technicians, distributors and private label nail brands.

    Non-ToxicHEMA-FreeVeganCEMSDSOEM / ODM
    Product TypeUV Gel Polish Remover
    Net Content15ml / 15g
    FormatBrush-On Liquid Gel
    Removal TimeApproximately 1-3 Minutes
    ApplicationSalon, Nail Technician, DIY
    Production Lead Time7-15 Working Days

    Key Benefits

    • Brush-on application helps simplify professional gel removal.
    • Gel layer lifts and peels without traditional foil wrapping.
    • Suitable for salon service, retail distribution and private label projects.
    • Custom formula, bottle, label, logo and retail box options available.

    Recommended Use

    1. Lightly file the gel top coat to break the sealed surface.
    2. Apply remover evenly over the cured gel, avoiding skin contact.
    3. Wait approximately 1-3 minutes for the gel layer to lift.
    4. Gently remove loosened gel and clean the nail surface.
