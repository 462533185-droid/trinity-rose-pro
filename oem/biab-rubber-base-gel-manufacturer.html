FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Key information regarding MOQ, formulations, compliance, and sample turnaround.

What is the minimum order quantity (MOQ) for private label BIAB and Rubber Base Gel?

For in-stock bottle shapes and standard catalog shades, our MOQ starts from 100 bottles (mixed shades allowed, typically 5-10 bottles per shade). For custom custom-colored bottles or bespoke formula development, MOQ is typically 500 to 1,000 bottles.

Do you offer HEMA-free and TPO-free BIAB formulas?

Yes, we formulate HEMA-free, HPMA-free, and TPO-free BIAB and rubber base gels compliant with EU Cosmetics Regulation (EC) No 1223/2009 and US MoCRA guidelines, complete with verified SDS and COA documentation.

Can I order BIAB gel in bulk 500ml or 1KG refill bottles?

Yes, we supply bulk 500ml, 1KG, and 1-gallon bottles for salons, training academies, and brands seeking refillable sustainable packaging models.

How long does it take to dispatch sample sets?

Standard sample sets (including popular cover nude, milky pink, and clear formulas) are typically dispatched within 3 to 5 business days with customer-arranged or factory-assisted courier delivery.
