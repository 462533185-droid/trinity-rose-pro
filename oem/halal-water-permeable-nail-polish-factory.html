FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Key information regarding Halal compliance, MOQ, formulations, and testing.

How does water permeability work in your Halal nail polish?

Our breathable formula utilizes an advanced microscopic porous polymer matrix that allows water molecules and oxygen to permeate through the dried lacquer layer to reach the nail surface, accommodating Wudu ritual requirements. Lab test video demonstrations are provided on request.

What is the minimum order quantity (MOQ) for custom branded Halal polish?

For existing bottle shapes and catalog shades, MOQ is 100 bottles (from 5-10 bottles per shade). For bespoke bottle shapes or custom color formulation, MOQ starts from 500 bottles.

Is the formula 100% vegan, cruelty-free and alcohol-free?

Yes, our Halal water-permeable nail polish is 100% vegan, contains zero animal-derived ingredients (such as carmine or guanine), zero prohibited harsh alcohols, and is cruelty-free.

What documentation and certificates are provided?

We provide comprehensive formula SDS (Safety Data Sheets), COA (Certificate of Analysis), facility ISO22716/GMPC documentation, and official order-bound certification support for customs clearance and market registration.
