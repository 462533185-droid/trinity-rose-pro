Quick Answer

What buyers need to knowHEMA-free means the identified product is formulated without HEMA as an intentionally listed ingredient. It does not automatically mean hypoallergenic, allergy-free or suitable for every person. Verify each base, color, top and builder formula separately.

Buyer questions

Does HEMA-free mean nobody can develop an allergy?

No. It describes an ingredient choice for a specific formula, not a guarantee against sensitivity or allergy.

Can the full collection be HEMA-free?

Selected options can be discussed, but every base, color, top and builder gel must be confirmed individually.

Is it automatically approved for the EU or US?

No single ingredient exclusion establishes complete compliance. Composition, labeling and market duties still apply.

Can HEMA-free combine with low MOQ?

Selected standard-formula projects may support smaller quantities; confirm the complete system, colors and packaging.
