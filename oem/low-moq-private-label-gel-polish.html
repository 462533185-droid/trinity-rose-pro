Quick Answer

What buyers need to knowFrom 100 bottles applies to selected projects using a suitable existing formula, standard bottle and practical decoration method. It is not a universal MOQ for every formula, color or custom box. Eligibility is confirmed after reviewing shades, quantity per shade and packaging.

What changes the minimum order

MOQ follows manufacturing and packaging setup, not only total bottle count.

Formula

Standard formulas are simpler; custom development and special materials can raise minimums.

Color Count

Many shades at tiny quantities are harder than a focused shade plan.

Packaging

Standard bottles and applied labels are simpler than custom molds, direct printing and boxes.

Artwork

Provide a vector logo and complete label copy before costing decoration.

Documents

Define testing and document requirements before approving the commercial scope.

Logistics

Compare landed cost including cartons, freight, duties and local handling.

Buyer questions

Can 100 bottles include different colors?

Mixed-color planning can be discussed, but the number of colors and quantity per shade affect feasibility.

Does the MOQ include custom boxes?

Not automatically. Boxes and special printing often have separate supplier minimums.

Can I use a custom formula at the lowest MOQ?

Custom formulation requires technical review and a project-specific minimum; the simplest route uses a confirmed standard formula.

How do I scale later?

Preserve formula codes, shade references and artwork, then reorder proven shades and add new colors in controlled groups.
