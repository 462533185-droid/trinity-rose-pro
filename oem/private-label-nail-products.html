A practical project workflow

A detailed written brief makes sampling and quotation more useful.

1. Define the market

Share target country, channel, customer and intended positioning.

2. Select the route

Choose stock, adjusted or custom formula and packaging.

3. Confirm samples

Check product performance, container and decoration.

4. Approve artwork

Finalize label copy, barcode, boxes and carton marks.

5. Produce and check

Manufacture against approved specifications and references.

6. Prepare shipment

Confirm cartons, documents and freight instructions.

Buyer questions

Do you work with startup nail brands?

Yes. Startups can begin with selected standard products and packaging, then add customization as demand becomes clearer.

Can categories share one brand design?

Yes. Bottles, labels, jars and boxes can be planned as one visual system while technical specifications remain product-specific.

What belongs in an RFQ?

Include target country, products, sizes, colors, quantity per SKU, packaging, logo method, documents, destination and target date.
