Buyer Information & Bulk Project FAQ

What is the minimum order quantity (MOQ) for bulk gels and powders? Standard bulk MOQ starts from 1KG or 500ml for in-stock formulas, and 5KG for bespoke custom shades. Repackaging into 15ml/30ml/60ml jars or bottles is supported from 500 units.

Are batch testing documents provided with bulk shipments? Yes. Every batch includes a 16-section REACH/OSHA-compliant Safety Data Sheet (SDS), Certificate of Analysis (COA), and heavy metal/microbial inspection records.

Are all formulas compliant with EU TPO ban and US MoCRA? Yes. Our bulk builder gels, rubber bases, and color gels are 100% TPO-Free (CMR 1B compliant) and HEMA-Free, meeting EU Regulation (EC) No 1223/2009 and US FDA MoCRA standards.

Can we customize viscosity, shade, and container packaging? Yes. Our lab can adjust dynamic viscosity, self-leveling speed, and cover pink/nude tones to match your master brand specifications.
