How to Use

Release a small amount of oil through the brush tip, then glide the brush over the cuticle and surrounding nail area. Gently massage until absorbed. Use as part of a daily nail care routine.

Storage: Keep tightly capped in a cool, dry place away from direct sunlight. For external use only.
