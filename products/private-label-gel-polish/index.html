Buyer questions

Can a new brand start with a small order?

Selected standard-formula and standard-packaging projects may begin from 100 labeled bottles. The practical MOQ depends on the number of colors, bottle choice, printing method and box requirements.

Can different gel colors be combined in one order?

Mixed-color planning can be discussed for selected small-packaging projects. Share the desired color count and quantity per color so the factory can confirm feasibility.

What documents can be provided?

Available support may include SDS, COA and ingredient information. Testing, CPNP or MoCRA-related preparation is evaluated according to formula, target market and project scope.

Are HEMA-free or TPO-free options available?

Selected formulas may be available with HEMA-free or TPO-free positioning. Buyers should confirm the final ingredient list, supporting documents and permitted claims for the destination market before sale.

How should I request a useful quotation?

Include target market, product type, bottle size, number of colors, quantity per color, packaging requirements, logo method, required documents and desired delivery timing.
