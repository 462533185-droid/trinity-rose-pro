Quality Control & Batch Traceability

Use the approved formula, sample, artwork and order specification as the reference for production and release.

Buyer note: A supplier QC report, factory photograph or video does not by itself establish formula identity, market compliance or future batch consistency. Confirm the exact product, evidence scope and release requirements for each project.

QC checkpoints

StageBuyer should confirmHold when

DocumentsProduct description, formula/batch references and destination-market documents.Required information is missing or inconsistent.

How to prepare a buyer QC brief

  1. Attach the approved sample and product specification.
  2. List formula, color, size, packaging and artwork revisions.
  3. Define the inspection stage, acceptance criteria and evidence required.
  4. Identify who may place an order on hold and who can authorize release.
  5. Record defects, corrective action and reinspection results before shipment.
