From idea to first order

1. Define the collection

Tell us your audience, target country, product type, color moodboard, reference shades, preferred size and the story behind the collection. You can start with Gel Polish, Builder Gel, Acrylic or Nail Art.

2. Develop the direction

Discuss your colors, formula direction, bottle, brush or jar, label and retail box. We can work from a color card, image, physical reference or a clear creative brief.

3. Review samples

Evaluate the selected product and shades on an approved sample. Confirm color appearance, application direction, packaging artwork and the details that matter to your customers.

4. Confirm the PI

After the product, packaging, quantity and destination requirements are agreed, the project commercial terms are confirmed in a written PI before the first order.

MOQ and first-order planning

There is no single MOQ for every signature collection. MOQ and small-batch feasibility depend on the product, formula, number of colors, bottle or jar, labels, boxes, quantity and destination requirements. We will confirm the applicable project terms before you decide.

  • Choose the product and number of shades;
  • Choose standard or customized packaging;
  • Confirm the quantity and target country;
  • Review sample results and artwork;
  • Receive the written PI for the agreed project.

Signature Nail Collection FAQ

Which products can be used?

Signature projects can be discussed across Gel Polish, Builder Gel, Acrylic and Nail Art products. The final product, formula and packaging are confirmed for the project.

Can I customize colors, bottles, labels and boxes?

Yes. Colors, bottles, labels and retail boxes can all be discussed for customization, subject to the selected product, artwork, quantity and project confirmation.

What is the MOQ?

MOQ is not one fixed number across all products. It is confirmed according to the selected product, formula, number of colors, packaging and project specification.

How are commercial terms confirmed?

After the product direction, packaging, quantity and destination requirements are reviewed, commercial terms are confirmed in a written PI for the project.
